# Product research protocol

Date: 30 October 2026. Single Codex-assisted reviewer; no independent duplicate screening. This is purposive discovery, not a completed systematic review.

The shared 200-paper foundation is separate. StockUp requires **100 relevant completed peer-reviewed critical appraisals and 20 qualified comparable-app workflow benchmarks**, followed by synthesis and comparative user/practitioner validation. These requirements remain unchanged.

Search inventory-record accuracy, cycle counting, receiving/issuing/transfer errors, identification, human factors, traceability, inventory-system adoption/usability and directly applicable replenishment decisions. Crossref metadata search uses eight recorded queries, 45 journal results per query, DOI deduplication and a 30 October 2026 publication cutoff. Supplement with primary publisher/institutional searches and citation chaining. Older foundational studies may qualify with explicit transfer limitations. This search is rank-limited, English-biased and cannot claim completeness.

Retain retrieved metadata separately from screened abstracts, available full text, completed appraisals and counted articles. Verify publication identity, article type, relevant peer-review provenance and corrections/retractions. Do not count metadata, abstracts, inaccessible articles, preprints, duplicate versions, correction notices or unrelated supply-chain work. Review papers count once; their bibliography does not count automatically. Assess corrected versions where applicable.

Each appraisal must record question, design, sample/context, measures, findings, bias/precision/causal limits, transfer judgments, requirements and decisive source locations. Read methods/results/limitations sufficiently for that appraisal. Distinguish simulation, observational associations, experiments and reviews. No automated metadata-to-appraisal substitution is permitted.

Choose 20 direct/adjacent substitutes with dated, denominator-aware adoption evidence; product-wide claims do not establish inventory-module use, and adoption does not demonstrate effectiveness. Selection is not a definitive top-20 ranking. Record version, tier, platform, language, access, rationale, task scripts and observed results. Official documentation starts desk research; it does not count as hands-on testing.

Common tasks: create/receive an item; find by SKU/barcode; issue and eligible return; start a blind count; introduce an intervening movement; review/correct discrepancy; transfer and receive; recover an error; restart; export and restore; keyboard/zoom/mobile inspection. Record unavailable steps explicitly. Do not create accounts, agree to terms, subscribe or contact vendors without applicable authorisation.

Before comparison, prespecify scenario, success, errors, steps/time, recovery and setup effort, order effects and meaningful improvement. Agent tests are engineering observations, never human validation. Include results that undermine a new app. Keep gates open when access, evidence or counts fall short.
